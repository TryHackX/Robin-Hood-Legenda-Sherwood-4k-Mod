Ready-made HD graphics for the SherwoodEngineFix mod — **no AI processing on your PC needed**. Requires the mod
**1.1.0 or newer** and the GOG edition of Robin Hood: The Legend of Sherwood.

## Installation
Unpack every archive you want **into the game folder** (the folder with `Game.exe`); they contain the `hd\` folder —
keep the paths. Then start the game; F6 / F7 switch the background variant, F8 compares with the original, F9 turns
the in-between animation frames on and off, F10 opens the mod's settings.

| Archive | Contents | |
|---|---|---|
| `SherwoodHD-1-postacie-a.zip` + `SherwoodHD-1-postacie-b.zip` | characters and all their animations, icons and markers | both needed |
| `SherwoodHD-2-tla-dat2.zip` | map backgrounds, roofs, trees, objects, menu backgrounds — variant `dat2` (default, most faithful) | needed |
| `SherwoodHD-3-klatki-posrednie.zip` | in-between frames of character animations (2× smoother animation, F9) | optional |
| `SherwoodHD-wariant-aomr.zip` | background variant `aomr` (contrasty, game-texture model) | optional |
| `SherwoodHD-wariant-sgi.zip` | background variant `sgi` (model for 90s pre-rendered game graphics) | optional |
| `SherwoodHD-wariant-ultrasharp.zip` | background variant `ultrasharp` (most added detail) | optional |

## What it is
The game's own pictures (all 20 maps, every sprite frame of the characters and objects, icons, menu backgrounds)
upscaled 2× with AI models, colours and brightness matched back to the original; the in-between frames were generated
with RIFE and checked automatically (pairs that failed the checks run as in the original game).

AI models: 4xNomos2_hq_dat2 and 2xAoMR_mosr by Phhofm (CC-BY-4.0), 4x SGI by ChrisNonyminus (GPL-3.0),
4x-UltraSharp by Kim2091 (CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 — the `ultrasharp` variant and the icons: non-commercial use only),
RIFE by hzwer et al. (MIT).

of no use without the game.
